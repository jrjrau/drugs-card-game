HOW TO PLAY

Goal: be the first to get rid of every card — your hand, then your 3 face-up cards, then your 3 face-down cards.

Setup

Everyone is dealt 3 face-down cards, 3 face-up cards on top of them, and a hand of 3. The deal is completely random — no choosing or swapping!

On your turn

  • Play a card equal or higher than the top of the pile. Several cards of the same rank can be played together.
  • Can't play? You pick up the whole pile.
  • After playing, draw back up to 3 cards while the deck lasts.
  • Hand empty (and deck gone)? Play from your face-up cards. Those gone? Flip your face-down cards blind — if the flip is illegal, you pick up the pile.

Special cards

  • 2 — resets the pile to 0. Play it on anything.
  • 3 — transparent: it counts as whatever card is underneath. Play it on anything.
  • 7 — the next player only must play 7 or lower (2, 3 and 10 still work).
  • 10 — kills the pile and you go again. Play it on anything.
  • J — reverses the play order (until the next Jack). Must be played legally like a normal card; playing several Jacks at once still reverses just once.

Each special has its own face, so you can spot them without remembering the list: the black bomb 10, the teal RESET 2, the frosted ANY 3, the amber MAX 7, and the violet REVERSE Jack.

Overdose

If the configured number of same-rank cards (default 4) pile up in a row on top, the pile overdoses — it's killed like a 10 and the player who completed it goes again. From three of a kind the pile catches fire, and when the flames turn red and start flickering fast you're one card short — the next of that rank kills it.

🃏 Chaos mode

Switch it on in the lobby and Jokers join the deck. A Joker can be played on anything, and leaves the pile wide open for the next player — but you never know what it does until you play it. Each one rolls a random effect:

  • Hand swap — you trade your whole hand with the next player.
  • Everyone passes — every hand moves one seat along.
  • Skip — the next player loses their turn.
  • Everybody pays — every other player draws a card.
  • Everyone dumps — every player throws their lowest card onto the pile.
  • Time bomb — the pile is armed: the next player eats it unless they play a 2, 3, 10 or Joker.
  • Gift — you choose one of your cards and hand it to somebody.

Jokers are never dealt into your face-up or face-down cards — they only reach you in your hand or from the deck. Several played together still roll just one effect.

Dropping out and watching

Lose your connection and a bot covers your seat — rejoin with the same room code and name (capitalisation doesn't matter) and you get your cards back. If everybody drops, the game pauses instead of ending, and waits a few minutes for someone to return.

Not playing? Enter a room code and hit 👁 Watch to spectate. You'll see the table and can chat, but not the players' hands.
